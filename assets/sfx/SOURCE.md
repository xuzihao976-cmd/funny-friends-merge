# Sound effects

Selected sounds from Kenney's [Interface Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds) and [Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds), both released under [Creative Commons CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Original OGG files were converted to mono PCM WAV for broad browser support.
